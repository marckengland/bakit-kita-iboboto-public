# Prepare a correction for the Senate guest pilot

Private report delivery is unavailable as of November 23, 2026. The sender domain
is not ready, and inbox delivery has not been verified. The `/contact` page shows
the disabled form. Keep your draft until a verified private channel is available.
There is no promised response time.

## Prepare the evidence

1. Copy the page URL and name the person, bill, or record at issue.
2. Quote the exact wording or identify the missing field. Record the date you saw
   it. State whether the issue concerns identity, a date, a citation, a factual
   statement, or private information.
3. State the change you request. Distinguish the existing statement from your
   proposed replacement.
4. For a factual correction, provide public source URLs, document names, dates,
   and the supporting page or passage. Explain any conflicting evidence. A filed
   bill does not establish enactment, impact, or a politician's career total. An
   allegation does not establish a finding.
5. Remove unnecessary private details. Do not include credentials, identity
   documents, or unrelated information about other people. For a privacy request,
   identify the information and requested action without repeating sensitive
   content unnecessarily.

Use this draft structure:

```text
Page URL:
Person, bill, or record:
Date observed:
Exact statement or field:
Requested correction or privacy action:
Supporting public source and page or passage:
Conflicting evidence or uncertainty:
Reply email, if wanted:
```

## Send only through a verified private channel

When private contact becomes available, select **Profile correction** or **Privacy
request** on `/contact`. A reply email is optional. The form accepts text and a
URL, without attachments. Read `/privacy` before sending details.

The form's enabled state means its configuration is present. It does not prove
mail delivery. An accepted submission means Resend accepted the email request,
not that the maintainer received or reviewed it. An error does not establish that
no provider copy exists. Avoid repeated submissions with additional private data.

Do not put private reports in general feedback or a public discussion. No
alternative working private channel is established by this document.

## Review before publication

A report does not update a profile or approve evidence automatically. A factual
correction needs the exact claim and sources checked against the
[evidence policy](EVIDENCE_POLICY.md). Contested allegations also require the
[case-review checks](CASE_REVIEW.md).

Prepare a new reviewed proposal for an accepted correction. Changing a database
row alone does not approve new evidence. The current Senate reader can withhold
changed rows that no longer match the approved batch. Preserve the supporting
citations, dates, uncertainty, and approval record through the existing
[data workflow](DATA.md).

Do not promise an acknowledgement, publication, removal, or a completion date.
Operator responsibility, triage access, retention, and response targets still
need approval before public operation. See the [privacy information](PRIVACY.md)
for how contact details pass through email and infrastructure providers.
