# Senate evidence refresh

Approved for integration on November 22, 2026. This version adds dated evidence to the
[original Senate batch](../senate-20/INDEX.md). It does not replace that batch or
certify current office and legislative status.

| Coverage                    | Addition                                                  |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| 24 profiles                 | Elected-term commencement and scheduled end, with sources |
| 24 profiles                 | A separate May 6, 2026 membership observation             |
| One leadership history      | Dated June and September 2026 observations                |
| 31 of the original 45 bills | 41 dated history entries                                  |
| Other 14 bills              | No approved additions in this version                     |

Twenty-two history entries are historical catalogue listings, mostly from July
and August 2025. Other entries include hearing agendas, a dated agency snapshot,
committee reports, and activity on separate substitute measures. A source
retrieval date is not an action date. An agenda does not establish a disposition.

## Read a term or a history entry

A scheduled elected term is different from actual tenure. The new term fields do
not establish uninterrupted service or current office. The original July 28,
2025 membership observation stays intact. The May 6 journal recognizes the same
24 people in that proceeding; it does not certify their position today or provide
a whole-term attendance rate.

A substitute is a separate bill. This version documents four exact relationships:

| Original | Recommended substitute | Committee report |
| -------- | ---------------------- | ---------------- |
| SBN-11   | SBN-1584               | 19               |
| SBN-82   | SBN-2115               | 59               |
| SBN-169  | SBN-1894               | 31               |
| SBN-189  | SBN-1432               | 5                |

The SBN-169 chain links to SBN-1894 and HBN-8476, approved as RA 12325 on
September 4, 2026. This does not mean original SBN-169 directly became law, that
every original provision survived, or that its introducer alone produced the law.
Publication, effectivity, implementation and social outcomes are not established
by that approval record.

All 45 original bills' current statuses and direct enactment remain unverified.
SBN-142 retains its conflicting filing-date observations and unknown canonical
date. Missing additions do not mean that a bill had no activity.

## Inspect the evidence

[data.json](data.json) contains each field or event's source IDs, exact locators,
source dates, retrieval or review dates, access method and source relationships.
Some official catalogue text was available through a search index while direct
access was blocked. Other parliamentary journals and the Official Gazette law
copy were retrieved directly. These access limits remain visible in the app.

The original filed document, a Senate catalogue page and a BetterGov copy are not
three independent origins. The app distinguishes source verification from a
check of its stored records. Read the [evidence policy](../../../docs/EVIDENCE_POLICY.md).

[Integration approval](APPROVAL.json) records the version and data hash.
[Reuse terms](DATA_LICENSE.md) cover the structured facts and original wording;
linked documents retain their own rights. Private research notes and source PDFs
are not bundled here.
