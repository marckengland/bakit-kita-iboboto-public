# Reviewed Senate profiles and selected filings

The Senate's opening roster for the 20th Congress, dated July 28, 2025,
supports the 24 membership observations in this batch. The batch also includes
45 selected filings and their field evidence.
The maintainer approved integration into the separate populated copy on November
19, 2026. Existing-database imports and publication require separate approval.
[Approval record](APPROVAL.json) · [Senate opening roster](https://legacy.senate.gov.ph/photo_release/2025/0728_13.asp)

The eight reviewed files preserve the proposal as it stood at review. The
proposal status in those files records that earlier stage. The approval record
documents the later integration decision and identifies the reviewed archive
by its SHA-256, `b8fbb6053bce3aa2a284d612fab490b85663abc52bb4fdfb7fda549075dc1faa`.

## Coverage

| Record              | What the batch establishes                                                          | What remains unknown                                                             |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Senate membership   | Publicly used names and membership observed on July 28, 2025                        | Current office and term dates                                                    |
| 45 selected filings | Bill references, titles, summaries, and introducers credited on the filed documents | Career totals, current legislative status, and enactment                         |
| Filing dates        | 44 supported dates                                                                  | The normalized date for SBN-142. Its details retain competing date observations. |
| Additional names    | Senate indexes list additional names for SBN-11, SBN-52, and SBN-82                 | Each additional name's role and addition date                                    |
| Three held filings  | SBN-102, SBN-141, and SBN-179 appear in the coverage record                         | The comparisons needed before inclusion remain incomplete                        |

Elections, party affiliations, attendance, votes, financial disclosures, family
relationships, and case claims fall outside this batch. Unknown fields supply
no basis for a performance score, a ranking, or a case finding.

The bill details identify the exact page, section, or index entry behind each
supported field. A Senate filing and a Senate index are records from the same
institution. BetterGov derives its entries from Senate records. A mirror or
indexed copy is another way to access a record.
