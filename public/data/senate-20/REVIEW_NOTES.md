## Field-level limits

Filing dates mean the dates recorded in explicit Senate filing entries, rather than an independently established first-submission date. Several filed-document stamps were absent or unclear in the accessible text. Those dates therefore have one readable primary field source even though the rest of the filing was cross-referenced. The candidate records that distinction instead of claiming two confirmations for every field.

Membership is a dated observation from the Senate's July 28, 2025 roster. BetterGov memberships and filed bylines corroborate the people and Congress, but they are not an independent roll call for that date. No term start, term end, or current-office value is supplied.

Titles can be catalog labels or the short title printed inside a proposed bill. The record states which one is used. Summaries describe proposals and make no claim about enactment or present law.

## Material excluded from this batch

The batch contains no full source documents, portraits, private contacts, case allegations, family links, financial figures, election tallies, voting records, or scores. The rest of the local research collection remains outside this proposed cohort pending its own review. It has not been deleted or classified as false.

Before app integration, unknown dates and statuses must remain representable. The importer must refuse values that the schema would otherwise invent through required fields or defaults. The data and its sources need approval as one exact version.
